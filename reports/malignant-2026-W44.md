## Summary

This cycle's *Blood*, *Blood Advances*, *Haematologica*, and *British Journal of Haematology* output is dominated by CAR-T biology/safety (transgenic T-cell malignancy, CD7 CAR-T EBV lymphoproliferative disease, exhaustion reversal), several practice-relevant myeloma CAR-T and transplant datasets, a large early-stage Hodgkin lymphoma comparative-effectiveness study, and real-world BTK-inhibitor dosing/outcome data in Waldenström macroglobulinemia. **(1)** In a phase 1/2 trial of concurrent BCMA (MCARH125) and GPRC5D (MCARH109) CAR T-cell infusion for relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma, coinfusion produced a 78% overall response rate with median PFS of 18.2 months, though dual-antigen targeting did not prevent antigen-loss relapse and paradoxically limited BCMA CAR T-cell expansion when co-administered with the GPRC5D construct[^1]. **(2)** A South Korean phase 1/2 study of anbalcabtagene autoleucel, a CD19 CAR-T with dual PD-1/TIGIT knockdown, reported a 67.1% complete response rate in relapsed/refractory large B-cell lymphoma (CRC01-01 trial, n=73 evaluable), with median PFS 6.04 months (95% CI 4.34–16.46) and 18-month OS of 57.3%[^2]. **(3)** A propensity-matched, 8-center analysis of 810 early-stage classical Hodgkin lymphoma patients found that omission of radiotherapy was associated with significantly inferior PFS in bulky disease (73.0% vs. 91.8%, HR 3.77, p=0.005) and interim-PET-positive disease (51.9% vs. 74.9%, HR 2.97, p=0.020), reaffirming a role for risk-adapted combined modality therapy despite overall five-year OS parity (97.3% vs. 98.6%, p=0.620)[^3]. **(4)** Real-world data on 236 Waldenström macroglobulinemia patients treated with zanubrutinib show that sub-standard initial dosing (<320 mg/day) independently predicted markedly worse outcomes (VGPR 14% vs. 39–40% with standard dosing, 30-month PFS 54% vs. 89%, HR for PFS 11.09, p=0.001), whereas toxicity-driven dose reductions after standard-dose initiation did not compromise efficacy[^4]. **(5)** A phase 2 study of tagraxofusp, azacitidine, and venetoclax in blastic plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm reported composite CR rates of 88% in first-line and 64% in relapsed/refractory patients, with 2-year OS of 65% and 2-year PFS of 53% in the first-line cohort, supporting this triplet as a new standard option ahead of transplant consolidation[^5].

## I. AML — Acute Myeloid Leukemia

Mechanistic and translational AML output continues to build on last week's mitochondrial and MRD themes with several genuinely new angles. A phosphoproteomic screen comparing normal HSPCs with leukemia-stem-cell-enriched AML populations identifies DEK phosphorylation at Ser301/303/306/307 (the "4S" sites) as a CK2-dependent event required for LSC maintenance in MLL-AF9– and HOXA9/MEIS1-driven AML models and patient-derived xenografts; phosphorylated DEK recruits GABPA to upregulate the oncogenic cofactor PBX3, and the clinical-stage CK2 inhibitor CX-4945 selectively depletes LSCs while sparing normal HSPCs, synergizing with venetoclax both in vitro and in vivo[^6]. A companion PROTAC study reports MA191, a VHL-based degrader of FLT3-ITD, inducing apoptosis in primary FLT3-ITD AML cells (IC50 9.6–11 nM) with degradation-based killing statistically superior to kinase inhibition alone (p<0.03); MA191 retains activity against tyrosine-kinase-domain resistance mutations and does not harm healthy hematopoietic progenitors or zebrafish larvae in vivo[^7]. A dual MYC/GSPT1 degrader, GT19630, is reported to disrupt a feedforward loop in which MYC drives GSPT1 transcription and GSPT1 in turn controls MYC translational termination; the compound shows nanomolar antiproliferative activity across AML, Burkitt lymphoma, and myeloma models including TP53-mutant lines, restores venetoclax sensitivity in venetoclax-resistant AML in vivo, and was well tolerated in humanized Crbn mice[^8]. Separately, a large Spanish/European nationwide study of donor cell-derived hematologic neoplasm (DDHN) across 24 transplant centers found a 0.28% prevalence among 8,465 allo-HSCT recipients, markedly higher after related-donor than unrelated-donor transplantation (0.41% vs. 0.06%, p=0.0026), with germline DDX41 variants identified as the predominant molecular driver (7 of 10 pathogenic germline cases), reinforcing recommendations for pretransplant germline screening of related donors[^9]. In pediatric severe aplastic anemia, enhanced whole-exome sequencing of 153 patients found cryptic inherited bone marrow failure syndromes to be rare (only 2 of 149 comprehensively worked-up patients harbored diagnostic or suggestive pathogenic variants), supporting prompt treatment without awaiting genetic results when standard clinical/laboratory evaluation is negative[^10].

## II. ALL — Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia

A new mechanistic study in infant KMT2A::AFF1-rearranged ALL demonstrates that the fetal oncogene LIN28B is essential for both normal human fetal B-lymphopoiesis and leukemic transformation: using a human fetal-liver-derived CRISPR model, KMT2A::AFF1 translocation failed to transform CD34+ cells in the absence of LIN28B, while LIN28B-expressing leukemias showed superior proliferation in vitro and in vivo, with this advantage lost upon LIN28B knockdown, nominating LIN28B as a therapeutic target in this dismal-prognosis subtype[^11]. In a large registry comparison of conditioning regimens for adult ALL undergoing first allo-HSCT (n=967), fludarabine-treosulfan was associated with significantly lower relapse incidence than fludarabine-melphalan (24.1% vs. 31.5%, HR 0.61, p=0.011) without an increase in non-relapse mortality, though overall and leukemia-free survival did not differ significantly between regimens, particularly benefiting B-ALL patients[^12].

## III. CML — Chronic Myeloid Leukemia

_No new signal this week_ (eIF4F/SBI-756 translation-complex mechanism reported previously[^13]).

## IV. CLL / SLL — Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia

Two *Leukemia* methodology papers caution against uncritical reliance on MRD as a CLL trial endpoint. A systematic review argues that measurable residual disease is not a universally reliable surrogate for PFS across novel-agent CLL trials, highlighting regimen-dependent discordance between MRD kinetics and long-term outcome[^14]. A complementary meta-analysis assessing concordance between bone marrow and peripheral blood MRD after first-line novel-agent therapy in CLL finds imperfect agreement between compartments, with implications for how MRD is measured and reported in future trials[^15]. In Richter transformation biology, immunogenomic characterization of 77 classic-Hodgkin-lymphoma-variant Richter transformation (CHL-RT) cases shows that Hodgkin/Reed-Sternberg cells in both type 1 and type 2 CHL-RT largely recapitulate the immune evasion phenotype of de novo classical Hodgkin lymphoma, though PD-L1 expression was less frequent in type 1 than type 2 disease (53.3% vs. 100%)[^16].

## V. MDS — Myelodysplastic Syndromes

A multicenter retrospective study of 511 patients with chronic myelomonocytic leukemia (CMML) validates an adapted IPSS-M for this disease, achieving a C-index of 0.678 for overall survival and 0.628 for AML progression across both myelodysplastic and myeloproliferative CMML subsets, with prognostic performance comparable to the recently developed international CPSS (iCPSS) and improved over CPSS-Mol, and an independent external validation of iCPSS in this genetically well-characterized cohort[^17]. A *Blood* review by Cazzola and Ogawa revisits splicing factor mutations in myeloid neoplasia 15 years after their discovery, emphasizing that mechanisms underlying their positive clonal selection and genotype-phenotype associations remain incompletely understood despite incorporation into current classification and prognostic schemes[^18].

## VI. MPN — Myeloproliferative Neoplasms (PV / ET / MF)

The German MPN Childhood Registry reports the largest prospective cohort of pediatric essential thrombocythemia (n=39) and polycythemia vera (n=6), finding that 69% of patients had at least one established adverse clinical marker at diagnosis (hyperviscosity symptoms or splenomegaly in 49% each); JAK2V617F was present in 44%, CALR in 18%, and 31% lacked a known somatic driver, among whom five patients carried novel candidate germline variants (including JAK2, THPO, FLT3, and SAMHD1 alleles) not previously described in pediatric MPN[^19]. In low-risk polycythemia vera, long-term follow-up data show deepening of JAK2V617F molecular response with low-dose ropeginterferon over extended treatment duration[^20].

## VII. DLBCL & Aggressive B-cell Lymphoma

Mechanistic work on CAR-T failure in large B-cell lymphoma shows that single-cell RNA-sequencing of post-CART19 progression tumors reveals clonally expanded but transcriptionally dysfunctional CAR-T populations with reduced IL2 signaling; the non-alpha IL2-receptor agonist pegenzileukin prevented this dysfunction in vitro and, in a patient-derived progression model, led to complete tumor eradication when combined with CART19, compared with transient response and CNS relapse with CART19 alone[^21]. A separate multi-omic spatial profiling study characterizes a newly described entity, post-CD7-CAR-T lymphoproliferative disorder (PCLD), showing that CD7-negative T cells with impaired antiviral function permit sustained EBV infection of B cells, with an immunosuppressive IDO1-high macrophage niche restricting T-cell infiltration via PD-1/PD-L1 signaling and enabling malignant B-cell transformation — a mechanistically distinct entity from classical post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder[^22]. A post hoc analysis of the RICOVER-60 trial examines beta-blocker use and survival associations in DLBCL, while frontline BTK-inhibitor-based induction in primary CNS lymphoma (n=131, IPTW-adjusted) was associated with improved PFS (HR 0.57, 95% CI 0.32–1.00, p=0.048) and higher complete response rate (OR 5.50, 95% CI 2.25–13.47, p<0.001) compared with conventional high-dose methotrexate-based regimens, though overall survival difference did not reach significance (HR 0.65, 95% CI 0.31–1.38, p=0.263)[^23].

## VIII. Indolent Lymphoma (FL, MCL, MZL)

A *Haematologica* perspective piece discusses BTK degraders combined with BCL2 blockade as an emerging therapeutic path in marginal zone lymphoma, following last week's real-world zanubrutinib data in this population[^24]. _No new quantitative efficacy data this week beyond prior zanubrutinib MZL report[^25]._

## IX. Hodgkin Lymphoma & T-cell Lymphoma

Beyond the Hodgkin lymphoma radiotherapy-omission analysis summarized above[^3], a real-world comparison of brentuximab vedotin-containing regimens in advanced-stage classical Hodgkin lymphoma examines time toxicity of condensed BrECADD versus traditional Bv+AVD in young adults and adults, an endpoint increasingly relevant to quality-of-life-oriented frontline regimen selection[^26]. In T-cell lymphoma immunology, a syngeneic ALK+ anaplastic large cell lymphoma mouse model demonstrates that immune surveillance is mediated by CD4+ T cells and NK cells, with PD-L1 blockade monotherapy inducing complete remission in approximately 50% of treated animals by reversing regulatory T-cell-mediated immunosuppression; nonresponder animals showed CD4+ T-cell exhaustion with a T-helper-22-like transcriptomic signature, providing preclinical proof-of-concept for checkpoint inhibition in ALCL despite heterogeneous clinical responses across T-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma subtypes[^27]. A small case series in North American adult T-cell leukemia/lymphoma reports that peginterferon alfa-2a plus bictegravir/emtricitabine/tenofovir alafenamide, with selective venetoclax, suppressed HTLV-1 viral load and reduced circulating ATLL burden, supporting prospective evaluation of this antiretroviral-based strategy in a disease with historically poor outcomes[^28].

## XI. Multiple Myeloma

Beyond the dual BCMA/GPRC5D CAR-T coinfusion data above[^1], a phase 1/2 trial of Evomela (propylene glycol-free melphalan) for autologous transplant conditioning in newly diagnosed myeloma (n=60) found MRD-negative stringent CR/CR rates of 45% at day 90 with comparable efficacy between short and long infusion schedules, and propensity-matched comparison suggested longer PFS versus standard melphalan 200 mg/m² (posterior probability of benefit 0.90)[^29]. An individual patient-level meta-analysis of six randomized trials (N=2,989) quantifies daratumumab-associated infectious risk in multiple myeloma, showing higher rates of any-grade (79% vs. 61%) and grade ≥3 infections (29% vs. 20%) versus non-daratumumab regimens, with risk peaking in the first 3 months (42% vs. 36%, p=0.001) and an adjusted incidence rate ratio of 1.60 (95% CI 1.47–1.73) for infection over 2 years; fewer than 20% of patients with severe respiratory infections received immunoglobulin replacement, highlighting an undertreated prevention gap[^30]. Dutch nationwide registry data describe 35-year trends in multiple myeloma incidence and survival in the Netherlands[^31], and a real-world analysis examines thromboprophylaxis use patterns during autologous HSCT for myeloma[^32].

## XII. Transplant & Cellular Therapy (CAR-T / HSCT / GVHD)

This week's *Blood* issue carries a dedicated review on CAR transgenic T-cell lymphoproliferative neoplasm, a rare but serious genomic safety signal following CAR T-cell therapy, proposing a multistep pathogenic model (pre-existing clonal fitness, vector integration, construct biology, postinfusion selection) and practical diagnostic criteria with proportionate genomic surveillance recommendations[^33]. In a pediatric allo-HSCT cohort, daily oral 2′-fucosyllactose (a human milk oligosaccharide) given from conditioning through day +30 preserved microbiome Shannon diversity compared with historical controls (p=0.04), and was associated with 0% incidence of grade II-IV acute GI GVHD versus 17% in controls (p=0.009) and 0% versus 17% adenovirus reactivation (p=0.009) in a phase 1/2 study of 70 patients[^34]. A Japanese registry analysis of allogeneic HSCT recipients found that deletion of chromosome 6p at diagnosis was independently associated with increased relapse risk (HR 1.30, 95% CI 1.02–1.65, p=0.036), with a more pronounced effect in HLA-mismatched transplants, consistent with attenuation of the graft-versus-leukemia effect through HLA-region loss[^35]. Immune reconstitution analysis across 210 patients receiving post-transplant cyclophosphamide (PTCy)-based GVHD prophylaxis shows delayed recovery of naïve T- and B-cell subsets and more persistent T-cell receptor repertoire restriction after haploidentical PTCy compared with HLA-matched conditioning, with matched-PTCy showing intermediate recovery kinetics[^36]. A low-cost, WHO-essential-medicine-based matched sibling BMT protocol for transfusion-dependent thalassemia (fludarabine, dexamethasone, busulfan, cyclophosphamide) achieved 97.3% engraftment and 93.4% thalassemia-free survival with only 3.9% moderate-to-severe acute GVHD across 176 pediatric patients in Asian and Middle Eastern centers, demonstrating that excellent outcomes are achievable in low-resource settings[^37]. Finally, a BMT CTN 1507 phase 2 trial of reduced-intensity haploidentical BMT for children with severe sickle cell disease (n=39) reported 2-year event-free survival of 79.3% (95% CI 62.8–89.1) and overall survival of 94.5% (95% CI 79.7–98.6), with resolution of glomerular hyperfiltration (eGFR decline from 174 to 127 mL/min/1.73m², p<0.001) and no new cerebral infarcts on serial MRI[^38].

## XIV. Taiwan Clinical Context

Zanubrutinib remains NHI-reimbursed in Taiwan for chronic lymphocytic leukemia and mantle cell lymphoma, and the new real-world Waldenström macroglobulinemia dosing data[^4] are directly relevant to NTUH and CGMH hematology clinics managing WM patients, where standard-dose initiation (160 mg BID or 320 mg daily) should be prioritized over empiric dose reduction given the markedly inferior PFS observed with sub-standard initial dosing. The tagraxofusp-azacitidine-venetoclax triplet for BPDCN[^5] is of particular interest for CMUH and VGH centers managing this rare, aggressive, CD123-driven neoplasm, though tagraxofusp availability and NHI reimbursement status in Taiwan should be confirmed before protocol adoption; capillary leak syndrome monitoring infrastructure is a prerequisite. The CMML-specific IPSS-M validation[^17] offers an actionable prognostic refinement applicable to Taiwanese MDS/MPN overlap registries at NTUH and CGMH. Given the relatively high prevalence of allogeneic HSCT for thalassemia historically performed in Taiwan, the low-cost matched sibling BMT protocol validated across Asian centers[^37] is directly transferable and may inform NHI cost-effectiveness discussions for pediatric transplant programs. The DDX41-driven donor cell-derived neoplasm data[^9] reinforce the case for pretransplant germline screening of related donors in Taiwanese transplant centers, particularly given regional registry efforts already underway at CGMH and NTUH for germline predisposition syndromes.

## XV. Key Takeaways

Dual-antigen CAR-T targeting in myeloma (BCMA plus GPRC5D) achieves high response rates but introduces novel resistance biology, including CAR-population competition for expansion — a finding with direct implications for sequencing strategy design[^1]. Safety biology of CAR-T therapy continues to mature, with this cycle contributing a formal nosology for CAR-transgenic T-cell lymphoproliferative neoplasms[^33] and detailed spatial characterization of EBV-driven lymphoproliferative disease after CD7 CAR-T[^22], both relevant to long-term surveillance protocols. In classical Hodgkin lymphoma, risk-adapted use of radiotherapy remains justified in bulky and interim-PET-positive disease despite the broader trend toward chemotherapy-alone approaches[^3]. Real-world BTK-inhibitor dosing data in Waldenström macroglobulinemia provide practice-changing reassurance that toxicity-driven dose reduction after standard-dose initiation is safe, while underdosing from the outset is not[^4]. Finally, infection risk with daratumumab-based regimens is concentrated in the first 3 months of therapy, arguing for front-loaded prophylactic strategies including immunoglobulin replacement in appropriate patients[^30].
